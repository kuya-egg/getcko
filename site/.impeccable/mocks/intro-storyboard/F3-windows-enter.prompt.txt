DESIGN SYSTEM (binding, do not deviate):
- Page ground is pure flat white #FFFFFF. Secondary wells #F6F7F4. Hairline borders 1px #E6E8E3. Text near-black ink #0E0F0C, secondary text #4A4D46.
- ONE accent: gecko green #39D86F, used only as solid fills (the mascot body, a small live dot, an icon-button fill). Green text is the darker #0F7A3D (links, citation chips with 1.5px #0F7A3D outline).
- Sun yellow #FFC83D appears ONLY as a single crisp 3px ring/halo around the ONE element the gecko is pointing at. Nowhere else.
- Absolutely NO gradients, NO glows, NO glassmorphism, NO neon, NO 3D renders, NO dark mode page, NO purple/blue. Flat, crisp, calm, generous white space. Shadows are very soft and subtle only on floating cards.
- Typography: display headlines in Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold (chunky, slightly quirky grotesque, very tight letter spacing); UI text in Geist (clean neo-grotesque); numbers and shortcuts in Geist Mono; one tiny pixel-font badge (Silkscreen) only for "Gets mo na." near the mascot.
- Buttons: primary = solid ink #0E0F0C fill, white text, 14px rounded corners, can hold a small dark keycap. Secondary = white with 2px ink border. Chips are pills.
- Mascot "GetCko": a flat 2D PIXEL-ART gecko made of chunky perfectly square pixels, thick black pixel outline, bright green #39D86F body, light green highlight, pale mint belly, small pink cheek, two big white square eyes with black pupils, standing upright and pointing up-right with one little arm. Crisp hard pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no shading gradients. Only ONE gecko visible per screen.
- Signature motion idea of this page is MORPHING: things dissolve into square pixels and reassemble as something else (shown frozen mid-morph as a stream/cascade of crisp square pixel blocks).
- Overall feel: as playful and confident as the "Not Boring Software" (notbor.ing) website, but executed with this restrained white + ink + one-green system. Award-winning marketing site craft.
